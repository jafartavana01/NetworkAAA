Authorization Policies

A policy is self-contained: it declares both when it applies (conditions below) and what it grants (privilege level + referenced Command Sets). Enabled policies are evaluated in ascending priority order — the first full match wins. Drag rows by the ⠿ handle to reorder priority — every policy below the drop point is automatically renumbered.

Conditions: leave a condition as "— Any —" to match everyone/everywhere on that dimension. A policy with no conditions at all matches unconditionally — useful as a low-priority catch-all.

Command rules now live on Command Sets, not here — a policy grants access by referencing one or more sets below. Manage the actual permit/deny rules on the Command Sets page.

On privilege levels: Cisco IOS only meaningfully distinguishes level 1 (user) and 15 (enable) out of the box. Intermediate levels only do anything if the device itself has custom privilege exec level mappings configured.
