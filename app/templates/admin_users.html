Admin Users

Who can log into this GUI, and with what privileges. Superadmins can manage other admin accounts and platform network/TLS settings; standard admins can do everything else.

Built-in safeguards: you can't delete or deactivate your own account, and the last remaining active superadmin can never be removed or demoted — both would risk locking everyone out of platform management. Restricting an account's trusted hosts is checked against your own current address if you're editing yourself, so you can't accidentally lock yourself out either.
