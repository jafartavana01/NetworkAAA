TACACS+ Sessions

Correlated from accounting records sharing the same device and port — start and stop records on the same device+port are paired into one session.

On "active": a session is only shown as active when a start record exists with no matching stop record yet observed in the current log — a genuine, data-backed observation, not an assumption. On correlation: tac_plus-ng has a real, protocol-level session identifier (task_id), but it turned out not to be usable in a custom accounting format string — a real deployment caught that with a fatal config error, and it was removed. Sessions here are correlated by (device, port) instead: a heuristic that assumes at most one open session per device+port at a time, true for the large majority of real deployments but not a protocol-level guarantee the way task_id would have been.
